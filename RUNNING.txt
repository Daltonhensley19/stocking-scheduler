STOCKING SCHEDULER
==================

To start the program, double-click:

    Windows:  StockingScheduler.exe
    Mac:      Stocking Scheduler.app
    Linux:    StockingScheduler

The first launch can take a few seconds while it unpacks — that's normal.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time because the
program isn't signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
On a Mac, right-click the app and choose "Open" the first time.

YOUR SCHEDULES
--------------
Everything you enter is saved automatically in a folder called "data" that
appears next to the program (the exact location is shown at the bottom of the
Help window). To back up or move to another computer, copy that folder.

Prefer a normal install with a Start-menu / Applications entry? Use the
StockingScheduler-Setup download instead of this zip.

If you keep the program on a shared drive, everyone who opens it from there
sees the same schedules.

QUICK START
-----------
1. Roster (left): Add your people; tick who is working today.
2. Drag over cells in the grid, then click a task on the left to fill them.
   Ctrl+T assigns one task to several people at once.
3. Print / Export: print the board, one page per person, PDF, PNG, or Excel.

Press "Help" in the toolbar for more.
